## 📜 Privacy Policy for Flying Football

**Effective Date:** 31 August 2025  
**Developer:** Akhtar Hasan

Flying Football is a mobile game developed by Akhtar Hasan. This Privacy Policy explains how the game handles user data and what choices players have regarding their information.

### 🔐 Information Collection and Use

Flying Football does **not collect any personally identifiable information** such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers.

However, the game may collect **non-personal data** to improve gameplay and performance. This includes:
- Device type and operating system
- Gameplay statistics (e.g., scores, progress)
- Crash logs and error reports

This data is used solely to enhance the user experience and improve game stability.

### 📊 Score Saving and Local Storage

The game stores your scores and progress locally on your device. This allows you to:
- Resume Endless mode sessions
- View leaderboards
- Save usernames for score tracking

No data is transmitted to external servers.

### 🔈 Sound Settings

Players can choose to enable or disable sound effects. This setting is stored locally and does not affect any personal data.

### 📢 Advertising

Flying Football may display banner ads using third-party services such as Google AdMob. These services may collect anonymized usage data to personalize ads and measure performance.

For more information, please refer to:
- [Google AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

### 🔒 Data Security

All locally stored data is kept securely on your device. The game does not access or transmit sensitive permissions such as location, contacts, or storage beyond what is required for gameplay.

### 🧾 Your Choices

- You can delete your saved data by clearing the app’s storage through your device settings.
- You can disable sound effects at any time via the in-game toggle.
- You may opt out of personalized ads through your Google account settings.
